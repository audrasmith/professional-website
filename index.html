I am a PhD candidate at the University at Buffalo, studying mechatronics and its intersection with swarm robotics.

My aim is to create a capable, but simplistic robotic platform by maximizing the capability of each individual robot. The design decisions at an individual level ultimately determine the capabilities and flexibility of the entire swarm. While many swarm platforms prioritize emergent behaviors above precise individual control, enabling controllable and directed locomotion and communication in a streamlined robot remains a critical step toward expanding the range of behaviors achievable in future collectives.
